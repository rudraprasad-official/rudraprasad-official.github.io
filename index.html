About Rudra Prasad

Rudra Prasad is an Indian hospitality engineering professional, Garhwali actor, filmmaker and humanitarian originating from Chamoli district in Uttarakhand, India.

His professional journey spans hospitality engineering, technical leadership, resort operations, regional music videos and community service.

Beginning his engineering career in July 2017, he progressed through hospitality establishments in India before moving into engineering leadership roles in the Maldives.

Beyond engineering, he has participated in Garhwali music videos as a lead actor and has undertaken film direction, production, choreography and cinematography.

Name and Identity

Rudra Prasad was known as Rakesh among his family and in his home community. His earlier official name was Bhagwati Prasad.

During his involvement in Uttarakhand's regional music industry, he appeared under the screen name Rakesh Rudra. Some earlier records and newspaper references also used Rakesh Pashwal.

In 2026, he legally changed his official name from Bhagwati Prasad to Rudra Prasad. Rudra Prasad is his current official name.

Hospitality Engineering Career

Rudra Prasad has developed his engineering career across Indian hospitality properties and luxury resort operations in the Maldives.

July 2017 — Career Beginning

Began his professional engineering career after completing a Diploma in Electrical Engineering in 2017.

Engineering Career in India

Gained hospitality engineering experience and leadership exposure across properties including Lemon Tree Hotels, Club Mahindra Corbett and Zone by The Park Gopalpur.

Engineering Career in the Maldives

Continued his career in international hospitality engineering, including experience associated with RAAYA by Atmosphere and the Maldives resort sector.

Engineering Leadership

Served as Senior Assistant Manager — Engineering at RAAYA by Atmosphere, supporting engineering operations, preventive maintenance, utilities, sustainability initiatives, technical projects and team management.

Professional Expertise

Electrical power distribution, diesel generators, HVAC and VRF systems, reverse osmosis plants, wastewater treatment, solar photovoltaic systems, fire and life safety, preventive maintenance, energy efficiency, technical procurement and hospitality engineering operations.

His academic background includes a Diploma in Electrical Engineering and a Bachelor of Technology completed in 2024.

Humanitarian & Social Work

Devbhoomi Online Blood Bank | 2015–2022

From 2015 to 2022, Rudra Prasad operated Devbhoomi Online Blood Bank, a nonprofit volunteer initiative focused on connecting patients with willing blood donors.

Through a volunteer network and telephone coordination, the initiative helped arrange blood donation support for more than 280 cases during its years of operation.

The initiative was covered in regional newspaper reporting, where his earlier name Rakesh Pashwal appeared.

The service operated as a donor coordination network rather than a physical blood storage facility.

Achievements & Recognition

2016 — Agriculture, Horticulture & Tourism Development Fair

Received recognition for participation in the 2016 Agriculture, Horticulture and Tourism Development Fair held in the Gairsain development block of Chamoli, Uttarakhand.

The certificate records a cultural programme performance and was issued under the name Rakesh Pashwal.

2019 — ASOZ Ceiling Fan Brand

In 2019, alongside his regular engineering employment, Rudra established a ceiling fan manufacturing venture in Gairsain, Chamoli, under the brand name ASOZ, associated with Devbhoomi Electricals.

The venture represented his practical interest in electrical product manufacturing and local industrial development.

Operations were discontinued approximately one year later due to the disruption associated with the COVID-19 pandemic.
