About Me

Rudra Prasad is an Indian hospitality engineering professional, Garhwali actor and filmmaker from Chamoli, Uttarakhand, India.

Previously officially known as Bhagwati Prasad, he was known as Rakesh among his family. He legally changed his name to Rudra Prasad in 2026.

Hospitality Engineering Career

Beginning his professional career in July 2017, Rudra has worked in engineering operations across India and the Maldives.

His professional experience includes Lemon Tree Hotels, Club Mahindra, Zone by The Park and RAAYA by Atmosphere, Maldives.

Garhwali Acting & Filmmaking

Rudra has performed in Garhwali music videos under the screen names Rakesh Rudra and Rudra.

  • Louti Aounla Pahada — Solo Lead Actor
  • Meri Nilima — Lead Actor with Rajni
  • Chhoti Syali Ludra — Lead Actor with Shashi Madhwal
  • Hey Suwa (2021) — Lead Actor with Priyanka
  • Meri Dagdya (2023) — Director, Producer, Choreographer & DOP

Humanitarian Work

Devbhoomi Online Blood Bank

280+ Patient Cases

From 2015 to 2022, Rudra operated a nonprofit online blood donor coordination initiative, helping connect patients with voluntary donors during medical emergencies.

Engineering & Manufacturing

ASOZ Ceiling Fans (2019–2020)

In 2019, Rudra established his own ceiling fan brand, ASOZ, through Devbhoomi Electricals in Gairsain, Uttarakhand, alongside his job. Operations ended around 2020 due to COVID-19 disruptions.

Cultural Recognition

Participated in a traditional folk dance performance at the Krishi Udyan Evam Paryatan Vikas Mela, Gairsain, in November 2016.
